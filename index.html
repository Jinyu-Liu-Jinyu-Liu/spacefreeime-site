How it works怎么工作

Two engines, one rule: never make you wait.两层引擎，一条规矩：不让你等。

01

You type one stream你只打一条流

Pinyin, English words, Spanish words, in whatever order they come out. No mode key, no mandatory spaces, no word-by-word picking.拼音、英文、西语，按它们冒出来的顺序打。没有模式键，不强制空格，不逐字选词。

02

The local engine answers first本地引擎先回答

Six local dictionary lanes run on your Mac and keep working without a network. We are validating the exact release data, source rights and long-run speed before opening a public installer.六条本地词典车道在你的 Mac 上运行，断网仍可使用。公开安装包开放前，我们正在核对最终词库、来源许可和长时间运行速度。

03

AI mode, on your own accountAI 模式，用你自己的账号

With a beta activation code from us and your own ChatGPT sign-in, longer sentences get rebuilt whole, with punctuation. Your text goes from your Mac to your own AI account and back. SpaceFreeIME runs no text-processing server.拿到我们发的内测激活码、登录你自己的 ChatGPT 之后，长句会整句重建、带上标点。文字从你的 Mac 到你自己的 AI 账号再回来。SpaceFreeIME 没有处理文字的服务器。

Leave the text alone when unsure.拿不准就不动。

Plain ASCII passes through untouched, and when the engine cannot decide it prefers your text as typed over its own guess.纯 ASCII 原样通过；引擎判断不了时，宁可保留你敲的原文，也不硬猜。

Password fields are off-limits.密码框不碰。

Secure text fields get the plain system keyboard. Nothing is composed, nothing is sent.安全输入框只用系统原生键盘。不组词，不发送。

Measure before release.先验证，再发布。

Candidate changes are compared on pinned accuracy and runtime checks. The public installer opens only after its release gates and Mac installation checks pass.候选改动会经过固定的准确率与运行检查。正式安装包要等发布门槛和 Mac 装机检查通过后才开放。

FAQ常见问题

Straight answers.直接回答。

Which languages and platforms?支持哪些语言和平台？

Today: macOS 13 or later on Apple Silicon. Chinese pinyin, English, Spanish, French, German and Japanese romaji can be mixed in one sentence. iOS, Android and Windows ports run the same engine and are in development; there is no date yet.目前：macOS 13 及以上，Apple Silicon。中文拼音、英语、西班牙语、法语、德语、日语罗马字可以混在一句里。iOS、Android 和 Windows 版跑同一套引擎，正在开发，还没有日期。

Does it need the internet?必须联网吗？

No. The local engine does everything offline. A beta activation code plus your ChatGPT sign-in adds the AI sentence mode for longer, mixed sentences.不用。本地引擎离线完成全部工作。有内测激活码并登录 ChatGPT 后，长句和混合句会多一个 AI 整句模式。

Can you read what I type?你们能看到我打的内容吗？

Ordinary local typing stays on your Mac. If you enable AI, the necessary input and context go directly to the AI provider you choose through your own account; SpaceFree does not proxy those model calls. Our invite service receives license requests, not typed text. Optional coarse product analytics will require separate consent and are not enabled for this public waitlist.普通本地输入留在你的 Mac 上。若启用 AI，必要输入和上下文会通过你自己的账号直接发给所选 AI 服务商；SpaceFree 不转发模型请求。我们的邀请服务只接收资格请求，不接收输入正文。可选的粗粒度产品统计需要单独同意，在这个公开等候页面上尚未启用。

Why sign in with my own AI account?为什么要用我自己的 AI 账号？

Because it keeps three things honest: your text stays yours, you keep your own account, model tier and limits, and we never have to raise the price to cover someone else's tokens. OpenAI accounts are supported today; other providers are not yet.因为这样三件事都干净：文字是你的，账号、模型档位和额度也是你自己的，我们也永远不用为了别人的 token 涨价。目前支持 OpenAI 账号，其他服务商还没有。

Intel Macs?Intel 的 Mac 能用吗？

Not yet. The beta ships for Apple Silicon only.暂时不能。内测版只提供 Apple Silicon 版本。

Is it patented?有专利吗？

Three U.S. provisional applications were filed pro se between September 2025 and May 2026, covering space-free whole-sentence decoding, provider-agnostic BYO-AI routing, and hardware-key-position layout decoding. The first has lapsed; two remain on file. Provisional applications are not granted patents and confer no enforceable rights.2025 年 9 月到 2026 年 5 月间自行递交了三项美国临时专利申请，涵盖无空格整句解码、不绑定服务商的 BYO-AI 路由、按键物理位置的键盘布局解码。第一项已失效，两项仍在案。临时申请不是授权专利，不产生可执行的权利。
